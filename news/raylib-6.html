Raylib 6.0 available in Jik packages repo

Jik now wraps Raylib 6.0. As a demo, Missile Defence is available here as a playable browser demo written with the new package.

Click inside the game to focus it, choose a difficulty with the left and right arrow keys, press Enter or Space to start, then click to launch your defences.

Game source: github.com/jik-lang/jik-packages/tree/main/packages/raylib/examples/missile_defence Raylib wrapper: github.com/jik-lang/jik-packages/tree/main/packages/raylib

Play in a new page: showcase/missile-defence/
